TL;DR. We already have the skill — it lives inside universal_agent as a project skill, newer than the video's install flow. Its differentiator is social relevancy: ranking by upvotes/likes/real-money (Polymarket) across walled gardens Google can't index, with a designed headless/cron engine path and native HTML/JSON/markdown emitters. We hold 2 of its useful keys (OpenAI, XAI); its free tier (Reddit + HN + Polymarket + GitHub) needs zero keys. The highest-leverage first move: a weekly Discovery-Mode "topic radar" feeding the intel pipeline and the Black Box channel's topic selection — one timer, evidence flows into the existing LLM briefing layer, no new pseudo-reasoning code.
